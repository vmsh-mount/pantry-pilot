# PantryPilot — Mission, Vision & Identity
*Last updated: 2026-06-25*

---

## Who We Are

PantryPilot is a household nutrition intelligence layer for Indian families.

We help households eat better and spend smarter on groceries — without making it a project. The planning, the optimisation, the ordering: we handle it. The household just lives better.

---

## Mission

Make nutritionally sound grocery planning effortless for every Indian household.

---

## Vision

Become the grocery brain for every Indian household — regardless of where they order from.

Today we fulfil through Swiggy Instamart. Tomorrow through Zepto, BigBasket, Blinkit, or wherever the household prefers. The intelligence is ours. The fulfilment rail is a plugin.

---

## What We Are

- **A household intelligence layer.** The core product is what we know about a household — their members, nutrition needs, pantry state, preferences, budget, and weekly rhythm. This is our asset, not Swiggy's.
- **A planner, not a reorder button.** We compose baskets the user wouldn't have thought of. We don't just replay history.
- **A quiet optimiser.** We make households healthier without lecturing them. The nutrition improvement is a side effect of a better plan, not a dashboard to feel guilty about.
- **A trust-first agent.** Automation is earned, not assumed. We start as an assistant and become an agent as the household trusts us more.

---

## What We Are Not

- **Not a Swiggy wrapper.** Swiggy Instamart is our first fulfilment partner. Our value lives above that layer.
- **Not a medical app.** We use ICMR-NIN RDA guidelines for healthy adults. We are not a dietitian, and we never claim to be.
- **Not a calorie tracker.** We don't ask users to log meals. We infer from purchases and ask only what we need.
- **Not a price aggregator.** We don't compare platforms or suggest buying elsewhere. We optimise within the platform the household uses.
- **Not an impulse engine.** We are not here to inflate cart size artificially. We are here to make the cart right.

---

## The Entity We Are Building Towards

A platform where:
- Any Indian household can connect their grocery preferences once
- PantryPilot plans, optimises, and places their grocery orders across any delivery platform
- The household profile, pantry state, and nutrition intelligence are fully owned by the user — portable, transparent, deletable
- Delivery platforms are partners, not gatekeepers

This is a meaningful company. Not a plugin.

---

## Our Relationship with Swiggy

Swiggy Builders Club is our launchpad. Swiggy Instamart is our first fulfilment rail. We are building with Swiggy, not inside Swiggy.

We respect their platform, honour their brand guidelines, and aim to drive genuine value for their users. In return, we build a product that stands on its own — one that makes Instamart more valuable, not more dependent.

---

## Guiding Principles

**Intelligence is our moat, not the integration.**
Any developer can call the Swiggy MCP. The household profile, nutrition model, and planning loop are what make PantryPilot worth using.

**Earn automation, don't assume it.**
We start as an assistant. We become an agent. Users should always feel in control — even when they're not doing any work.

**Quiet wins over loud.**
A household that eats 18% better without knowing we optimised their cart is a bigger win than a dashboard they open once and forget.

**Platform-agnostic by design.**
Every architecture decision should ask: would this work if we added a second fulfilment partner? If not, rethink it.

**Honest about what we are.**
We are not a medical product. We are not a fitness app. We are a grocery planning tool that happens to care about nutrition. Stay in that lane.
